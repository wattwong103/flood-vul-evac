  it requires re-staging and a new eight-run set.

  remain explicitly `unknown`, and known-band shares are covered-cell shares.
- The city layer has no current result. Geofabrik staging contains 71% more road
  ways and 57% more buildings than the old BBBike cache, but neither inventory
  is a ground-truth completeness measure.
- The verified ZIP is a local Dropbox evidence copy, not a durable independent
  archive.
